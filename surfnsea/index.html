The Brand

Surf N Sea, established in 1965 in Haleiwa, Hawaii, is a historic surf shop that has long been a landmark in the small town, attracting visitors from around the world. While the current logo is recognizable, it feels outdated by today’s design standards.

Logo Process

I wanted the new concept to reflect the original logo while emphasizing the importance of proper gear for water activities. Inspiration came from local duck sightings and their connection to surf terms.

Digitization

Through multiple iterations, I refined various elements of the logo and achieved a more balanced sense of proportion.

Refinement

Negative and positive space in the design was essential to reinforcing the visual balance of the logo.
