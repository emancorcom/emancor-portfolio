The City

Along the French Riviera lies Monte Carlo, Monaco, renowned for its luxury lifestyle, the Casino de Monte-Carlo, and glamorous events like the Formula 1 Grand Prix. It attracts visitors with its stunning Mediterranean coastal views and resorts.

Taking

A

Gamble

Three Concepts

After research, brainstorming, and sketching, I developed multiple logo concepts with strong designs and meaningful connections to the city.

Street Lamp

Ornate lamps are scattered throughout the city streets, providing both lighting and a unique atmosphere, making them a prominent feature of the city.

Umbrella

Located on the Mediterranean coast umbrellas have become a common sight. Given the history and significance of the grand casino, a poker chip combined with an umbrella’s canopy makes for a fitting and unique combination.

Palms

Palm trees are scattered throughout the city and can often associate with wealth. Monaco gained its prosperity through the grand casino, and the image of a poker chip combined with a palm leaf represents this connection.

Right

Choice

Logo Process

I chose the most visually unique logo to make it distinctly representative of Monte Carlo.

Digitization

To create a proportional logo, I designed a geometrically accurate umbrella while referencing a poker chip.

Refinement

Smoothing the shapes with curves enhances the logo’s elegance. This softens the overall design, making it feel more inviting and visually balanced.
