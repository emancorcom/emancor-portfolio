About Me

I’m a graphic and web designer based in Honolulu, Hawaiʻi, with a strong foundation in design principles developed through the New Media Arts program at Kapiʻolani Community College. I specialize in creating visually compelling designs through an efficient and effective workflow. As a dedicated and fast learner, I’m always looking for opportunities to expand my skill set—especially when working with new tools and programs. I’m committed to pushing my creative limits to consistently deliver high-quality work.

Experience

During my time in the New Media Arts program, I had the opportunity to intern at WASISWAS under the mentorship of Bradley Shin. This experience helped me apply symbolism and visual storytelling to my design work. It was a valuable part of my creative development, strengthening both my creativity and the expertise needed to make impactful and meaningful designs.
